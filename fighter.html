All about fighters

Fighters are durable close-range heroes who combine strong attacks with the ability to survive in extended battles.

Fighters released in chronological order

  • 2016
    Balmond
    BalmondThe Bloody Beast is a durable fighter who uses spinning attacks and an execution ability to finish enemies.
  • Alucard
    AlucardThe Demon Hunter excels at chasing targets and restoring health through powerful physical attacks.
  • Freya
    FreyaThe Valkyrie uses shields, leaps, and enhanced attacks to dominate close combat.
  • Zilong
    ZilongThe Spear of Dragon uses mobility and rapid attacks to pursue vulnerable enemies.
  • 2017
    Ruby
    RubyThe Little Red Hood controls fights with wide crowd-control skills and built-in spell vamp.
  • Jawhead
    JawheadThe Steel Sweetheart throws enemies and uses powerful rockets to initiate fights.
  • Roger
    RogerThe Dire Wolf switches between ranged human attacks and close-range wolf attacks.
  • 2018
    Aldous
    AldousThe Contractor gathers stacks to deliver devastating punches and track targets across the map.
  • Leomord
    LeomordThe Oath of Twilight charges into battle on horseback and attacks with powerful area damage.
  • Thamuz
    ThamuzThe Lord Lava thrives in sustained combat with scythes, burning damage, and regeneration.
  • Badang
    BadangThe Tribal Warrior uses his fists and summoned barriers to trap and demolish enemies.
  • Guinevere
    GuinevereThe Magic Swordswoman combines mobility, magic damage, and airborne crowd control.
  • 2019
    Terizla
    TerizlaThe Executioner swings his massive hammer to deal heavy area damage and slow enemies.
  • X.Borg
    X.BorgThe Firaga Armor uses fire-based attacks and an explosive ultimate to pressure groups.
  • Masha
    MashaThe Wild-oats Fist has multiple health bars and excels at split pushing and dueling.
  • Dyrroth
    DyrrothThe Prince of the Abyss breaks defenses and overwhelms opponents with enhanced attacks.
  • 2020
    Yu Zhong
    Yu ZhongThe Black Dragon uses the Sha Essence to sustain himself and transform into a dragon.
  • Khaleed
    KhaleedThe Desert Scimitar controls sand to charge, heal, and strike enemies across the battlefield.
  • Barats
    BaratsThe Dino Rider grows larger through his passive and uses crowd control to disrupt enemies.
  • Benedetta
    BenedettaThe Shadow Ranger uses dashes and precise sword strikes to evade and punish opponents.
  • 2021
    Paquito
    PaquitoThe Heavenly Fist chains empowered punches and dashes to control close-range fights.
  • Phoveus
    PhoveusThe Astaros uses enemy dashes as opportunities to leap into battle and punish mobile heroes.
  • Aulus
    AulusThe Warrior of Ferocity upgrades his weapon and gains power during extended fights.
  • 2022
    Arlott
    ArlottThe Lone Lancer marks enemies and combines precise strikes with powerful crowd control.
  • 2023
    Cici
    CiciThe Buoyant Performer uses her yo-yo and mobility to harass enemies from a safe distance.
  • 2024
    Suyou
    SuyouThe Wielder of the Immortal uses his dual forms and powerful weapons to adapt to every fight.
  • 2025
    Lukas
    LukasThe Beast of Light combines martial arts with a powerful transformation to overwhelm opponents.
  • 2026
    Sora
    SoraSora uses agile close-range attacks and transformative power to control the battlefield.
