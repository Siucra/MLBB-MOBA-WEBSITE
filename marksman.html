All about marksmen

Marksmen deal physical damage from range. They are often weak early game but can change the direction of a match in the late game.

Marksmen released in chronological order

  • 2016
    Miya
    MiyaThe Moon Priestess is a classic marksman who uses rapid arrows and stealth to overwhelm enemies.
  • Bruno
    BrunoThe Protector uses powerful kicks and enhanced shots to deal critical physical damage.
  • Layla
    LaylaThe Energy Gunner attacks from extreme range and becomes more dangerous as she levels up.
  • Clint
    ClintThe outlaw combines firearms and quick-draw skills to punish enemies with burst damage.
  • Yi Sun-shin
    Yi Sun-shinThe Legendary Admiral switches between ranged and melee attacks while commanding his turtle ship.
  • 2017
    Moskov
    MoskovThe Spear of Quiescence attacks through enemies and can stun them against walls.
  • Irithel
    IrithelThe Jungle Heart can move and fire at the same time while riding her lion companion.
  • Karrie
    KarrieThe Lost Star specializes in shredding the health of durable enemies with true damage.
  • 2018
    Lesley
    LesleyThe Deadly Sniper uses camouflage and critical shots to eliminate targets from a distance.
  • Claude
    ClaudeThe Master Thief attacks quickly with the help of his partner Dexter and a mirror image.
  • Hanabi
    HanabiThe Scarlet Flower uses bouncing kunai and crowd control to damage groups of enemies.
  • Kimmy
    KimmyThe Chemical Ranger fires while moving and combines physical and magical damage.
  • 2019
    Granger
    GrangerThe Death Chanter uses a powerful gun and music-inspired skills to burst down targets.
  • Wanwan
    WanwanThe Agile Tiger exposes enemy weaknesses and attacks while constantly moving.
  • 2020
    Popol and Kupa
    Popol and KupaThe duo fights together, combining ranged attacks with Kupa's powerful abilities.
  • Brody
    BrodyThe Lone Star delivers heavy attacks and marks enemies before finishing them.
  • 2021
    Beatrix
    BeatrixThe Dawnbreak Soldier switches between four weapons to adapt to any battle.
  • Natan
    NatanThe Spacetime Walker uses a boomerang and a reverse-time clone to deal mixed damage.
  • 2022
    Melissa
    MelissaThe Cursed Needle fights with enchanted needles and dolls that link her attacks to enemies.
  • 2023
    Ixia
    IxiaThe Siphon uses dual weapons and a wide ultimate to dominate large areas of the battlefield.
  • 2025
    Obsidia
    Obsidia"The Sovereign of Dark's End", a ruler of darkness, now seeks to slay the God of Darkness and hunt down its Spawns of Decay.
