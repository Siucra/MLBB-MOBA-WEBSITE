Preparation for your battle

Before you start a match, it's important to prepare your hero with the right emblems, talents and equipment to gain an advantage over your opponents.

Emblems & Talents

Emblems are hero upgrades that give extra bonuses and effects. You can pick your emblem set in the preparation tab or during the hero pick phase. There are currently 7 types of emblems, each with its own bonuses.

Talents come in two kinds: standard talents and core talents. They give your hero extra effects, so the right setup can give you an edge. You can choose two standard talents and one core talent. There are currently 16 types of standard talents and 8 types of core talents, each with its own bonuses.

Equipment

Also known as an item, is an enhancement to a hero's abilities that can be purchased in a match using gold. Using the shop interface the equipment can be obtained at the top right corner. Each player can keep up to six pieces of equipment.

Most equipment gives heroes bonuses. Some boost a hero's stats, while others grant special abilities. Bonus attributes raise a hero's stats and stack if you buy the same item more than once. Unique attributes also raise stats, but only one copy of the effect applies per piece of equipment, no matter how many you buy. Unique passives are always active while the item is equipped, like a hero's passive skill. Unique actives work like hero skills: tap the equipment icon next to your hero skills to use them, then wait for the cooldown to end before using them again.

Builds

Using the information from the previous sections, you can create effective builds for your hero. A build is a combination of preset equipment and talents that work together to provide a specific playstyle or strategy. You can either create your own build for your hero or use the default build provided by the game, or alternatively use builds from other players. Its good practice to have a few different builds ready for different situations.
