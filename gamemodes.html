Game Modes

There are several game modes available in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang. Each mode offers a unique gameplay experience. This section will provide a brief overview over the core modes that make up this game. All of these modes, whilst having their own unique features and strategies, share one main objective: Distroy the enemy base.

Classic

The Classic mode is the first and earliest game mode and all other game modes base their rules on it. It is a 5v5 battle where rank is unaffected but winrate is affected. Once a hero is selected, you cannot unequip your choice. Each team cannot have the same hero twice. Classic mode is a common ground for players to practice heroes or play for a more relaxed experience.

Ranked

Ranked mode is the most competitive mode in the game. Similar to classic in terms of layout, ranked mode features a more intense and strategic gameplay experience. Players are matched based on their skill level and performance. Once a hero is selected, you cannot unequip your choice. Additonally, both team cannot have the same hero twice. If you lose a match, your rank will be affected. From epic rank and above, players will be placed in a draft pick where heroes are selected in a specific order after banning other heroes. Each player must select a role, unlike classic where you could have the same role twice like 2 mages, although unpreferable.

There are 10 different ranks:

Warrior Starting rank for new players. Elite The first step beyond Warrior. Master A rank for developing players. Grandmaster Players begin refining their strategy. Epic Draft pick begins at this rank. Legend A highly competitive rank. Mythic The highest standard of ranked play. Mythical Honor A prestigious level of Mythic play. Mythical Glory Reserved for elite competitors. Mythical Immortal The pinnacle of ranked achievement.

Brawl

In this gamemode players are given a choice of 2 random heroes to play with. Fights in this mode happen only in one lane with two turrets defending the the base in both teams. The base does not heal and each hero either has a buff or a debuff. You can only purchase items when dead or leaving the respawn area for a very short time.
