All about tanks

Tanks are durable heroes who absorb damage, protect their teammates, and control the battlefield with powerful crowd-control abilities.

Tanks released in chronological order

  • 2016
    Tigreal
    TigrealThe Warrior of Dawn protects allies with shields, knock-ups, and powerful initiation.
  • Akai
    AkaiThe Panda Warrior uses his heavy body and spinning ultimate to disrupt enemy formations.
  • Franco
    FrancoThe Frozen Warrior catches enemies with his hook and suppresses them with devastating control.
  • Minotaur
    MinotaurThe Minotaur uses rage to heal allies and launch enemies with a powerful ground-shaking ultimate.
  • 2017
    Lolita
    LolitaThe Steel Elf protects her team with a shield and stuns enemies with her hammer.
  • Johnson
    JohnsonThe Wild Engine transforms into a car to transport allies and crash into enemies.
  • Gatotkaca
    GatotkacaThe Might of the Gods turns received damage into strength and leaps into fights from afar.
  • 2018
    Hylos
    HylosThe Grand Warden creates a path for allies and uses his reserves of energy to endure combat.
  • Uranus
    UranusThe Aethereal Defender regenerates quickly and becomes stronger as he absorbs damage.
  • 2019
    Belerick
    BelerickThe Guard of the Enchanted Forest protects allies and reflects attacks back at enemies.
  • Khufra
    KhufraThe Tyrant of the Desert controls movement with bouncing attacks and long-range crowd control.
  • Baxia
    BaxiaThe Mystic Tortoise rolls through battle and reduces enemy regeneration with his defenses.
  • 2020
    Atlas
    AtlasThe Ocean Gladiator uses his chained mech and ultimate to gather enemies for a devastating attack.
  • Grock
    GrockThe Fortress Titan gains defenses near walls and creates terrain to protect his team.
  • 2021
    Gloo
    GlooThe Swamp Spirit splits into blobs and attaches to enemies to control and weaken them.
  • 2022
    Edith
    EdithThe Ancient Guard shifts from durable tank to ranged damage dealer through her powerful transformation.
  • 2023
    Fredrinn
    FredrinnThe Rogue Appraiser stores damage as combo points and converts them into powerful attacks.
  • 2024
    Chip
    ChipThe Prodigy uses portals to connect the map and helps his team rotate into unexpected fights.
