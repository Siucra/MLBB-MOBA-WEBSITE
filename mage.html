All about mages

Mages are a class of hero in MLBB, they deal magic damage but are typically fragile. There are many different types of mages.

List of all mages released in chronological order, starting from 2016 up to 2026

  • 2016
    Alice
    Alice"Queen of Blood", the oldest mage in the game with a rich lore and multiple art reworks. When played right, Alice can be an unstoppable force in the battlefield.
  • Nana
    Nana"The Sweet Leonin", the only hero that is capable of transforming enemies. Her poke ability and unique passive that gives her a second chance makes her a one-of-a-kind on the battlefield.
  • Eudora
    Eudora"The Lightning Weaver", a powerful mage with a strong presence. Known for her swift ability to control the battlefield and her devastating ultimate.
  • Gord
    Gord"The professor of the Mystics", who rides a floating board and delivers magic and true damage to his enemies.
  • 2017
    Aurora
    Aurora"The Frost Queen", a powerful mage with a strong presence. Known for her swift ability to control the fight and her devastating ultimate.
  • Vexana
    VexanaThe Necromancer Queen commands cursed forces and summons the undead to overwhelm her enemies.
  • Harley
    HarleyThe Magic Mathematician is a clever young mage who uses cards, portals, and explosive tricks in battle.
  • Odette
    OdetteThe Swan Princess uses enchanting melodies and elegant magic to control the battlefield.
  • Zhask
    ZhaskThe Planes Dominator is an alien ruler who calls upon his Nightmaric Spawn to invade the battlefield.
  • Pharsa
    PharsaThe Wings of Vengeance is a powerful aerial mage who rains long-range spells upon her foes.
  • 2018
    Kagura
    Kagura"The Onmyouji Master", The radiant bloom of her Seimei umbrella delivers a deadly blow to her opponents.
  • Cyclops
    Cyclops"The starsoul Magician" who has his eye on you with is ranged combo attack will have you dead before you see him.
  • Lunox
    Lunox"The Twilight Goddess" The first mage of her type to have 2 different combinations depending on her playstyle, attack and defense.
  • Harith
    Harith"The Time Traveler" A quick-witted apprentice of magix
  • Valir
    ValirThe Son of Flames wields destructive fire magic and fights to master the power within him.
  • 2019
    Kimmy
    KimmyThe Chemical Ranger combines science and combat training with a spray gun that fires while she moves.
  • Chang'e
    Chang'eThe Moon Palace Immortal is a playful mage who uses moon magic and a rabbit companion to bombard her enemies.
  • Esmeralda
    EsmeraldaThe Astrologer manipulates shields and starlit magic to absorb enemy defenses.
  • Guinevere
    GuinevereThe Magic Swordswoman blends aristocratic grace with powerful jumps and spell-enhanced attacks.
  • Lylia
    LyliaThe Little Witch uses magical bombs and her Shadow Energy to confuse enemies and escape danger.
  • Bane
    BaneThe Lord of the Seven Seas is a pirate captain who uses rum, cannon fire, and crushing tide magic.
  • 2020
    Cecilion
    CecilionThe Embrace of Night is a refined blood demon who casts powerful spells and protects his beloved Carmilla.
  • Luo Yi
    Luo YiThe Yin-yang Geomancer commands the opposing forces of Yin and Yang to manipulate enemies and allies.
  • Vale
    ValeThe Windtalker controls the wind with precise spells and shares a long rivalry with Valir.
  • 2021
    Yve
    YveThe Astrophysicist is an alien guardian who channels cosmic energy to stop the Swarm.
  • Aamon
    AamonThe Duke of Shards is a masked assassin who uses enchanted armor and fragments to vanish and strike.
  • Valentina
    ValentinaThe Prophetess of the Night can borrow the ultimate abilities of her enemies through forbidden magic.
  • 2022
    Xavier
    XavierThe Defier of Light is a young Arbiter who fires long-range light magic at enemies from afar.
  • Julian
    JulianThe Scarlet Hound is a cursed fighter who combines chained weapons and magic in flexible attacks.
  • 2023
    Novaria
    NovariaThe Star Rebel is a celestial wanderer who fires star-powered attacks across great distances.
  • 2024
    Zhuxin
    ZhuxinThe Beacon of Spirits guides lanterns filled with souls and uses them to control the battlefield.
  • 2025
    Zeitan
    ZeitanA powerful empress who channels formidable power against her enemies.
