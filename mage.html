All about mages

Mages are a class of hero in MLBB, they deal magic damage but are typically fragile. There are many different types of mages.

List of all mages released in chronological order, starting from 2016 up to 2026

  • 2016
    Alice
    Alice"Queen of Blood", the oldest mage in the game with a rich lore and multiple art reworks. When played right, Alice can be an unstoppable force in the battlefield.
  • Nana
    Nana"The Sweet Leonin", the only hero that is capable of transforming enemies. Her poke ability and unique passive that gives her a second chance makes her a one-of-a-kind on the battlefield.
  • Eudora
    Eudora"The Lightning Weaver", a powerful mage with a strong presence. Known for her swift ability to control the battlefield and her devastating ultimate.
  • Gord
    Gord"The professor of the Mystics", who rides a floating board and delivers magic and true damage to his enemies.
