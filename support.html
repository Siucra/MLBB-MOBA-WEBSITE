All about supports

Supports provide utility to their teammates through healing, shielding, vision, mobility, and crowd control.

Supports released in chronological order

  • 2016
    Rafaela
    RafaelaThe Wings of Holiness heals allies, increases their speed, and uses light magic to stun enemies.
  • 2017
    Estes
    EstesThe Moon Elf King links to allies and restores their health with powerful healing magic.
  • Diggie
    DiggieThe Timekeeper uses bombs, vision, and a team-wide control cleanse to protect his allies.
  • 2018
    Angela
    AngelaThe Bunnylove attaches to allies, healing and shielding them while slowing enemies with puppet strings.
  • Kaja
    KajaThe Nazar King uses lightning and Divine Judgment to pull key enemies out of position.
  • 2019
    Faramis
    FaramisThe Soulbinder summons a spectral domain that lets allies return to battle and fight again.
  • 2020
    Carmilla
    CarmillaThe Shadow of Twilight steals defenses and links enemies so damage and control spread between them.
  • Mathilda
    MathildaThe Swift Plume guides allies to safety and combines mobility with protective spirit magic.
  • 2021
    Floryn
    FlorynThe Wandering Bloom heals allies anywhere on the map and grants them an extra source of power.
  • 2022
    Edith
    EdithThe Ancient Guard protects allies as a tank and transforms into a ranged damage dealer.
  • 2025
    Kalea
    KaleaThe Tidecaller supports her allies with water-based abilities, healing, and powerful control.
  • 2026
    Marcel
    MarcelMarcel helps his team with supportive abilities and strategic control during team fights.
